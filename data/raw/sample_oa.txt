2. Clarity (Art. 84 EPC)
Claim 1 refers to a "dynamic scheduling offset". This term is considered vague. In the field of 5G NR, it is unclear whether this offset relates to the $K_0$ parameter (PDSCH) or the $K_2$ parameter (PUSCH). The applicant is requested to clarify the technical scope.

3. Inventive Step (Art. 56 EPC)
The subject-matter of independent claim 1 does not involve an inventive step for the following reasons:
D1 discloses a method for resource allocation in a wireless network where a User Equipment (UE) receives Downlink Control Information (DCI) indicating a frequency resource.
The difference between the subject-matter of claim 1 and D1 is the specific mapping of the "hybrid-ARQ feedback timing".
However, D2 (3GPP TS 38.211) already describes the standard procedure for HARQ-ACK codebook construction.
A person skilled in the art, starting from D1 and seeking to optimize feedback efficiency in a New Radio (NR) context, would find it obvious to apply the teachings of D2 to the system of D1.
Therefore, the combination of D1 and D2 renders the subject-matter of claim 1 obvious.
