Title: Telecom Response Template A — Art. 84 EPC (Clarity) for 5G/NR Terms

Context
Typical objections under Art. 84 EPC arise when claim terms are broad or lack objective boundaries in a telecom context (e.g., “user equipment”, “resource”, “offset”, “PUCCH configuration”).

Core response logic
1) Identify the exact unclear expression
- Quote the term(s) from the claim and the passage(s) in the Office Action.
- Explain why the term is objectively clear in the technical context, or why additional technical limitations are appropriate.

2) Provide objective technical boundaries
- Replace subjective language with measurable parameters (e.g., define “offset” via a reference point, index, or mapping table).
- If relevant, specify the protocol layer (PHY/MAC/RRC) and the entity (UE/gNB) responsible.

3) Anchor terms to the standard where appropriate (without importing limitations)
- Mention standard terminology to clarify meaning (e.g., 3GPP TS 38.213, 3GPP TS 38.331).
- Clarify that the claim defines the feature by functional-technical constraints rather than by merely citing the standard.

4) Proposed amendment strategy
- Add one or two concrete constraints that the skilled person can verify from the claim alone.
- Ensure support in the description (basis for amendment) and consistency of reference signs (if any).

Suggested wording (examples)
- “The term ‘user equipment’ is a well-established term in the art. For avoidance of doubt, the claim is amended to specify that the UE is configured to perform <STEP> at the PHY layer in accordance with a configured <PARAMETER>.”
- “The alleged ambiguity is resolved by defining the offset Δ with respect to <REFERENCE> and by specifying the mapping between <INDEX> and <RESOURCE>.”

Checklist
- Claim language provides objective boundaries
- Consistent entity responsibility (UE vs gNB)
- No added matter introduced by the clarification amendment
